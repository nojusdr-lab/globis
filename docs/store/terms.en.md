## What GLOBIS is

GLOBIS helps you find animals waiting for a home in Lithuanian shelters. The app shows information that shelters have published on their own websites. GLOBIS is not a shelter: it does not keep, sell or decide on the adoption of any animal.

## Animal information

- Data (name, photos, age, status) comes from shelter websites and is refreshed once a day. It may be out of date or inaccurate.
- If a shelter does not state a fact, the app does not guess and shows it as unknown.
- Always confirm with the shelter before visiting an animal. The shelter has the final word.
- Adoption terms, contracts and fees are set by the shelter. GLOBIS is not responsible for them.

## Using the app

- You can browse and save animals without an account. The app is free.
- You may create an optional account so your saved list is available on several of your devices. You are responsible for keeping your password safe.
- You can delete your account at any time in the app: Account → "Delete account".
- Not allowed: disrupting the app or its servers, bulk automated downloading of data from GLOBIS, using the app for fraud or animal trading.
- If these terms are broken, we may suspend or delete the account.
