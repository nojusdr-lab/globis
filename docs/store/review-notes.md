# App Review pastabos (anglų k., įklijuoti į App Store Connect)

**1. Screen recording:** [Attach a recording from a physical iPhone: launch → swipe cards → open profile → save → Saved tab → Shelters → About.]

**2. Purpose:** GLOBIS helps people in Lithuania find dogs and cats to adopt from animal shelters. It brings shelter listings into one mobile app, lets people save a shortlist on their device, and links to the shelter's original listing to arrange adoption.

**3. Access:** No account or sign-in is required. All features are available immediately. There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions, ads or remote feature flags.
- Open **Globoti** (Discover) to browse cards. Swipe or use the **Išsaugoti** (Save) / **Praleisti** (Pass) buttons. Tap a card to open the profile.
- **Filtrai** (Filters) narrows by species and city.
- **Išsaugoti** (Saved) tab lists saved pets; they stay on the device.
- **Prieglaudos** (Shelters) lists shelters with search.
- The language button in the top bar (shows **LT**) switches the interface to English.
- Tap the GLOBIS logo for **About**, with privacy and support information.

**4. External services:** Pet photos load from public shelter/listing websites. "Original listing" opens the shelter's website. No analytics, ad, crash-reporting, AI or authentication services are used.

**5. Regional differences:** The app works the same in every region. Content covers shelters in Lithuania.
