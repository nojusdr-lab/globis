**1. Screen recording:** [Attach a recording from a physical iPhone: launch → swipe cards → open profile → save → Saved tab → Shelters → Account → sign in → delete account.]

**2. Purpose:** GLOBIS helps people in Lithuania find dogs and cats to adopt from animal shelters. It brings shelter listings into one mobile app, lets people save a shortlist on their device, and links to the shelter's original listing to arrange adoption.

**3. Access:** No account is required. Browsing, filters and saving work immediately without signing in. An optional account (email and password) only syncs the saved list across devices. There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions, ads or remote feature flags.
- Open **Globoti** (Discover) to browse cards. Swipe or use the **Išsaugoti** (Save) / **Praleisti** (Pass) buttons. Tap a card to open the profile; tap the left or right side of a photo to see more photos.
- **Filtrai** (Filters) narrows by species and city.
- **Išsaugoti** (Saved) tab lists saved pets.
- **Prieglaudos** (Shelters) lists shelters with search.
- **Paskyra** (Account): language (Lietuvių / English), About, optional sign-in.
- Account deletion (guideline 5.1.1(v)): Paskyra → sign in → **Ištrinti paskyrą** (Delete account) → confirm. The account and its saved list are deleted at once.
- Demo account for review: [create one in Supabase before submitting and paste the email and password here].

**4. External services:** Pet photos load from public shelter/listing websites. "Original listing" opens the shelter's website. Listings and the optional account use Supabase (EU, Frankfurt). The account stores only the email address and the IDs of saved animals. No analytics, ad, crash-reporting or AI services are used.

**5. Regional differences:** The app works the same in every region. Content covers shelters in Lithuania.
